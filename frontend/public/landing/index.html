Before you begin

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Understand where CaseProof fits—and where human judgment remains essential.

Does CaseProof replace the analyst?

No. It organises evidence and surfaces supported next actions. Staff review the material and an authorised reviewer approves supported handoffs.

Does a delivered packet mean money is frozen?

No. Acknowledgement records delivery. It does not establish a hold, freeze, refund, regulatory filing or closed investigation.

Will this work with our existing bank systems?

CaseProof’s local workflow demonstrates the case lifecycle with bank data and simulator endpoints. A real rollout would require agreed interfaces, staff identity, delivery contracts and operational validation. Compatibility with a specific bank has not been established.

What does the customer see?

A guided reporting journey, evidence upload, report status and focused clarification requests through scoped reply links.

Is this connected to live banks or Google?

Our deployed staging workflow uses Google Document AI and Vertex AI Gemini for evidence processing. Practice bank records and receiving endpoints are synthetic. Each assessment identifies its processing provider. Institutional bank connections and staff sign-in are separate integrations; a practice identity does not establish production access.

Can I test a fresh complaint?

The integrated local Studio supports fresh practice sessions and new supported complaints. Choose Explore a practice case to open the live reporting journey. The illustration above remains a labelled example.

How would pilot pricing work?

Commercial terms have not been published. Scope would need agreement around evaluation goals, users, data, integration work and operational requirements.
