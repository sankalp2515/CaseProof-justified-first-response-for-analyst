How this local demo works

An employee opens the complaint. The customer adds evidence through a private link. Local OCR and rules extract cited facts; bank matching and tracing use simulated data. A reviewer approves before simulated reports are sent.

The demo case includes ₹48,274 and ₹25,000 debits with a fixed replay clock. No real SMS, bank report, freeze or refund is performed.
